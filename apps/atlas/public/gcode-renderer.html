The renderer makes the execution order inspectable.

It is deliberately a preview layer, not machine-control software. The parser understands the commands used by these fixtures: rapid moves, linear cuts, clockwise and counter-clockwise arcs, laser enable/disable, and pass comments.

InputThe actual manifest and GCode files in this delivery; no invented geometry.
PlaybackMoves remain in original file order. A selected layer filters the same ordered records rather than generating a new cut path.
ExportThe download is the exact selected `.gcode` fixture, not canvas output or a regenerated approximation.
BoundaryNo controller connection, no post-processor, no material calibration, and no physical-result claim.

Use it as a bounded preflight

  1. Choose one of the three fixture programs.
  2. Use a layer button to inspect the corresponding plate’s sequence and design role.
  3. Play or step through the toolpath; the status panel shows the source command now under the playhead.
  4. Open the matched SVG for a static comparison, or download the exact fixture for your own reviewed workflow.
  5. Before any machine use, bind a tested material profile and controller-specific safety procedure outside this viewer.
