# Ritual Geometry: Intelligent Design Plan

**Status:** operating proposal; local strategy artifact; no customer, ad, checkout, controller, or physical-fabrication claim is implied.

**Read this first:** Ritual Geometry is not a generic "AI art generator," a laser-control replacement, or an unbounded mystical-content brand. It is becoming a **proof-led design system for layered geometric artifacts**: people can see the family lineage, understand each layer's job, inspect the intended assembly, and take away a bounded digital or fabrication-ready-for-review artifact.

## The north star

> A person sees a layered object become intelligible in seconds, wants one in their hands or in their space, and can trace the route from idea to artifact without being asked to believe a black box.

The product has three linked but separately valuable loops:

1. **Discovery loop:** an image, short motion, or static composition makes the layered object desirable.
2. **Understanding loop:** a visitor sees that every layer differs, understands the assembly story, and sees honest proof/status rather than fake authority.
3. **Action loop:** a buyer, educator, maker, or collaborator can choose a bounded offer with clear delivery, limitations, and next action.

The system wins when these three loops reinforce one another. Beautiful without proof is easily copied. Proof without desire is a lab notebook. A clean offer without either becomes another fragile download page.

## What exists now

### Observed, local repository state

- A dependency-free public atlas and browser GCode renderer exist under `apps/atlas/public/`.
- Three authored, sequential GRBL 1.1 draft fixtures exist: Quiet Bloom (5 layers), Frequency Bloom (7), and Leaf Spiral (5). Every pass has distinct interior geometry, shared registration, ordered toolpaths, matching SVG preview, and a manifest hash.
- The local-only Proof Rail increment links Quiet Bloom's selected GCode, served MachineJob, source SHA-256, frame, registration choreography, layer IDs, and draft evidence state. It is deliberately not a controller or physical-test claim.
- The source architecture already separates authored family identity (Ritual Geometry) from later machine/process execution (Omni-Loom). That separation is a strategic advantage, not paperwork.

### Not observed; do not imply

- No dated material/kerf profile, controller setup, physical overlay measurement, customer research, paid conversion, workshop run, resale rights package, payment flow, ad account, or deployed commercial site has been validated.
- The local Proof Rail changes are not yet committed or pushed. The public remote must not be described as containing them until a reviewed commit is pushed.

### Immediate architecture reconciliation flag

The decision package's narrative release law says `draft → advisory → release`, while the current Family and MachineJob JSON schemas allow `draft | release | withdrawn` and do not include `advisory`. This is an actual vocabulary incompatibility, not a styling issue. Do **not** silently add a state in one surface. Before any release workflow, commercial copy, or physical-evidence promotion is built, record a compatibility decision that names:

1. the canonical state set and transitions;
2. what `advisory` means operationally, if retained;
3. whether `withdrawn` remains a terminal archival state;
4. the migration/versioning rule for existing draft fixtures; and
5. the exact tests and public wording that change with the decision.

The current safe behavior remains `draft` plus the specific evidence label `static_pass; physical_unrun`.

## The decision: a proof-led studio-commerce hybrid

### The four paths

| Path | Intention | Benefits | Risks | Why it is not the first whole answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Digital layer-pack shop** | Sell individual visual kits, printables, SVGs, or assembly guides. | Fastest path to an offer and buyer language; low operational burden. | Commodity-download trap; unclear machine safety if GCode is oversold. | Valuable entry point, but needs the Proof Rail and lineage story to avoid becoming clip art. |
| **B. Full fabrication SaaS** | Become authoring, material setup, controller, output, support, and collaboration software. | Large theoretical surface area; repeat use. | Competes directly with mature control tools; safety/support debt arrives before demand. | Too wide now. LightBurn and xTool already cover much of the control/material workflow. |
| **C. Workshops / curriculum / cultural installation** | Sell facilitated making, educational packs, commissions, and physical experiences. | Strong regenerative value, higher ticket, strong story and recognition. | Delivery depends on facilitation, physical evidence, and local partners. | A great second revenue engine after one repeatable artifact has proved itself. |
| **D. Evidence and interoperability layer** | Package provenance, geometry, proof, and handoff contracts for other design/fabrication tools. | Deep differentiation; reusable across products; supports Omni-Loom later. | Abstract value is hard to sell alone; enterprise work can stall. | Keep as the spine, not the first product sold. |

### Recommendation

Build **A + C on top of D**, in that order:

1. A beautiful, finite, purchasable/registrable *Layer Stack Field Kit* makes the system legible and tests demand.
2. The same exact proof, lineage, and assembly assets become an educator/workshop kit and eventually commissioned spatial work.
3. The contract/evidence architecture accumulates underneath, without asking individual buyers to care about schemas.

This is not a compromise. It is a flywheel:

```text
distinct visual reveal
      -> interest / save / share
      -> field kit or inquiry
      -> actual assembly, workshop, or commission evidence
      -> stronger proof, stories, source material
      -> better family catalogue and higher-value offers
```

## The first customer and the first promise

### Beachhead customer

**Design-forward maker / art director with access to a laser, printer, workshop, or fabrication collaborator.** They value a composition that already has a visual language and an assembly logic. They do *not* need a universal controller application.

Why this person first:

- They can understand the value of a named family and layered relief immediately.
- They can use an SVG/PDF/assembly guide before a mature machine-integration product exists.
- Their projects create the photographs, feedback, and variants that later enable workshop and B2B offers.

### First promise

**"Make a layered geometric object that reads as an authored composition, not five copies of the same cut file."**

The public promise is art + construction clarity. The private implementation promise is traceability. Do not lead marketing with Bessel terminology, GCode, a graph, or claims about frequency. Those are supporting receipts for people who want to inspect the work.

### v0 visual target: decided for speed

The first Field Kit page inherits the already accepted local renderer language: dark ink/navy field, bone paper counterpart, restrained gold for authored value, mint for verified static structure, vermilion only for blockers, precise monospaced proof data, and the existing exploded-stack reference. This is a **continuity decision**, not a claim that the current renderer itself is a finished commerce page. It lets RG-01 begin without a brand-reset detour. A later motion/3D study may deepen the spatial experience only after the offer page proves its basic communication job.

## Demand discovery and marketing plan

### The content atom

One 12–20 second visual loop, rendered in a way that can be used as a Reel, Short, Story, web hero, and landing-page section:

```text
flat line field -> layers separate -> each layer names its contribution -> stack settles -> final relief
```

The rule: motion teaches registration and contribution. It must not imply vibration, self-assembly, magic, material fusion, or a physical result that was not observed.

### Three creative angles to test

| Angle | Hook | Visual evidence | CTA | Signal it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Every layer has a job** | "Five plates. Five different reasons to exist." | Exploded-to-assembled motion; one highlighted distinct geometry per beat. | See the full stack. | Is depth/composition the desire trigger? |
| **From line to object** | "A pattern is not a product until it knows how to become one." | Path, registration, assembled-view progression. | Get the Field Kit brief. | Does proof create trust and intent? |
| **A family, not a file** | "One visual language, many honest views." | Coloring/stencil/stack as siblings with lineage label. | Explore Quiet Bloom. | Is provenance/collectibility a differentiator? |

### Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Asset shape | First action | Do not do yet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short-form video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) | Discovery and taste. | 9:16 assembly loop with clear title card. | Click to proof/brief page. | Spend on ads before the landing event is instrumented. |
| Pinterest / visual search | Evergreen visual discovery. | High-resolution exploded stack, final object, process pin. | Save / click to field kit page. | Treat impressions as demand. |
| Maker and design communities | Credibility through usefulness. | A transparent proof post, not promotional spam; answer a concrete fabrication question. | Volunteer for five feedback sessions. | Drop unrelated links or claim controller support. |
| Email / collector list | Permissioned follow-up. | A useful assembly/lineage mini-guide. | Choose maker, educator, or commission interest. | Long automated funnels before audience language is known. |
| Partner outreach | Physical trust and higher-ticket work. | One-page studio capability sheet + exact project boundaries. | 20-minute discovery call. | Promise a production backend or hand a draft GCode to an unknown machine. |

### Paid discovery rule

No paid ads until the page records a meaningful action and the asset can make the promise in the first two seconds. Then run a small, capped **creative test**, not a scaling campaign: one audience, three angles, one destination, one agreed spending cap, and a stop date. Success is not reach; it is the relative rate of *qualified brief requests* and the comments/interviews explaining why.

### Hypothesis gates

| Gate | Smallest responsible test | Continue when | Stop / change when |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1: visual pull | 3 creative variants to a single landing page. | One angle clearly leads qualified actions after a predeclared sample window. | Saves/views rise but no one asks for the object, kit, or workshop. |
| G2: offer clarity | 10 target conversations + a click-to-brief page. | People can repeat what they get and choose one offer without explanation. | The user asks "what is this?" after seeing the reveal. |
| G3: willingness | Limited founding offer, manual fulfilment. | 3 paid pilots or equivalent signed workshop/commission intent. | Only compliments, no time/money/introductions. |
| G4: physical trust | One documented material/profile test. | One honest test record can accompany the artifact. | Results expose transform, bridge, registration, or safety failures. |

## Architecture: the shape to keep, the shape to defer

### Keep

```text
Ritual Geometry (authored meaning, family, views, visual proof)
  family schema -> geometry kernel -> constraint/evidence records -> packs
  public atlas / offer surface -> renderer / proof rail
                             -> versioned MachineJob handoff
Omni-Loom (later process, adapter, material, machine, and release consumer)
```

The MachineJob boundary stays small and versioned. The atlas does not become a controller. Omni-Loom does not absorb authored family identity. This keeps marketing intimacy on one side and machine risk on the other.

### Optimize next

1. Promote the current proof checks into a reusable **Evidence Record** shape shared by all three fixtures, not a one-off Quiet Bloom UI rule.
2. Add an **Offer Record** and **Experiment Record** that point to the same `family_id`, source asset, claim, and metric. This is how marketing becomes auditable rather than a separate mood board.
3. Add a **Claim Register** with `observed | computed | proposed | unknown`, source pointer, owner, and `reopen_if`. It makes the system self-describing without magical self-trust.
4. Keep the present static app through the first demand test. Do not incur a React/Next migration merely to feel more serious.

### Defer deliberately

- Full machine control, camera overlays, material database, universal GCode export, marketplace, subscriptions, login, payment, server-side social automations, vector database, GraphRAG, and Unreal/Unity runtime integration.
- Any "autonomous self-improvement" that can revise source truth, publish, advertise, change release state, spend money, or rewrite a contract without human review.

## Knowledge graph: make it a garden, not a hallucination machine

The first graph is a local, versioned **project graph**. It is not a claim that the system is sentient. It is a way to notice relationships without losing the source trail.

### Initial node and edge vocabulary

```text
Nodes:
  Family, View, LayerStack, MachineJob, SourceAsset, GeometryFixture,
  MaterialProfile, EvidenceRecord, Claim, Decision, Experiment,
  Audience, Offer, CampaignAsset, Integration, Component, Skill

Edges:
  derives_from, renders, emits, requires, constrained_by, evidenced_by,
  asserts, supports, invalidates, tests, offers, targets, uses, blocks,
  supersedes, candidate_for
```

### Ambient intelligence contract

Every automatic observation may create only a **candidate insight**. A candidate must carry: source references, time, confidence, explanation, affected decision, and a recommended reversible next action. It cannot alter a claim, release state, deployment, offer, ad, source file, or human commitment. A human promotes, dismisses, or reopens it.

The first useful "ambient" pulse is modest and powerful: detect a stale source hash, an unfulfilled prerequisite, an offer with no linked evidence, a decision whose `reopen_if` fired, or a work order that is blocked for missing inputs. This is vastly more valuable than asking an LLM to invent relationships from a tiny folder of documents.

### Technology sequence

| Stage | Choice | Why | Gate before advancing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | JSON records + deterministic local audit script. | Portable, inspectable, zero secret/config debt. | 25–50 source/decision/experiment records with stable IDs. |
| 2 | SQLite graph tables plus a read-only graph view. | Real queries, stable snapshots, low operational risk. | A recurring question cannot be answered from the ledger alone. |
| 3 | Optional embeddings / retrieval. | Helps find related artifacts, never replaces source pointers. | Human evaluation shows it surfaces useful links above a baseline search. |
| 4 | GraphRAG experiment in a separate, budgeted sandbox. | Useful for corpus-wide questions and community summaries, not source truth. | Corpus, model budget, prompt policy, cache, evaluation questions, and deletion policy exist. |
| 5 | Durable scheduled pulse service. | Detects stale links, failed builds, and candidate opportunities. | A real schedule, destination, ownership, and notification threshold are approved. |

Microsoft's GraphRAG documentation itself warns that indexing can consume substantial model resources and requires tuning; it is a later analysis tool, not the first database. [GraphRAG overview](https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/) · [cost/setup warning](https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/get_started/)

## Motion, visual technology, and engines

### What to use when

| Need | Best next choice | Reason | Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact GCode truth | Existing Canvas/DOM renderer. | Small, inspectable, fast, no framework migration. | Full 3D scene. |
| Premium exploded-stack experience | A contained React Three Fiber / three.js scene only after the offer-page visual direction is chosen. | Interaction, camera, layer focus, and state map cleanly to reusable components. [R3F](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/) is a declarative three.js renderer. | Making R3F the foundation of every page. |
| 2D high-density particles, line fields, and transition effects | PixiJS experiment when Canvas profiling demonstrates a real need. | Strong asset caching and 2D rendering workflow. [PixiJS assets](https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/assets) | Rebuilding an existing good canvas in a heavier engine. |
| Advanced web shaders | Three.js WebGL baseline; optional WebGPU/TSL spike behind feature detection. | The official WebGPURenderer falls back to WebGL 2, but its renderer is still described as experimental. [three.js WebGPU guide](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/webgpurenderer) | Betting the product on WebGPU or HLSL portability. |
| Unity / Unreal | Export a versioned geometry/motion specification plus meshes/textures later. | A shared data contract travels better than pretending shaders or scenes are portable. | Building native-engine runtime before web demand, assets, or physical specs exist. |

HLSL, WGSL, GLSL, Unreal materials, and Unity shaders can learn from the same **motion intent** (layer order, reveal timing, material state, camera path), but they should not be treated as interchangeable source files. Share a neutral `MotionSpec` and geometry assets; compile/author the engine-specific material separately.

### Desired visual outcome

The signature visual is not "more effects." It is a **calm, surgical reveal**: linework breathes into separated plates, camera moves through the gaps, each plate briefly owns the screen, then all plates seat into one relief. Grain, shadow, light, and motion should clarify depth and assembly. Reduced motion must leave the exact same explanatory information as static before/after views.

## Research evidence and conclusions

### Observed

- LightBurn's current documentation presents a broad laser-control stack with material library, camera, positioning, optimization, and machine-management functions. Its material libraries are made from operating presets and can be shared. [LightBurn material library](https://docs.lightburnsoftware.com/UI/MaterialLibrary.html) · [LightBurn camera calibration](https://docs.lightburnsoftware.com/2.2/Reference/Cameras/)
- xTool's current Studio release notes describe material presets, processing preview, vector workflows, and a shift from Creative Space to Studio. [xTool Studio release notes](https://support.xtool.com/article/1773)
- React Three Fiber offers a declarative React interface to three.js; three.js's WebGPURenderer has WebGL2 fallback but is still experimentally positioned; PixiJS currently documents a stable 8.x line and cache-aware assets. [R3F](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/) · [three.js WebGPU](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/webgpurenderer) · [PixiJS versions](https://pixijs.com/versions)
- GraphRAG extracts entities, relationships, claims, communities, and reports from a corpus, but its own documentation warns that indexing has meaningful model-resource costs. [GraphRAG](https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/)
- Cloudflare Workflows can persist/retry multi-step processes and wait for external events, which is a plausible later implementation for a reviewed pulse system; it is not needed to begin the local operating ledger. [Cloudflare Workflows](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/workflows/)

### Inferences

- A direct machine-control contest is a weak use of current scope; an interoperable, proof-led family-and-assembly layer is a stronger wedge.
- The highest-fidelity visual investment is the exploded stack/assembly reveal because it is both the product's distinctive image and its best educational interface.
- The first real commercial test should sell one small promise around a named family, not software access, a full catalogue, a generic subscription, or an unverified fabrication guarantee.

## Operating system and agent handoff

The durable sources of truth are:

1. `ops/ritual-geometry-work-orders.json` — machine-readable priorities, gates, inputs, outputs, and no-go constraints.
2. `ops/2026-09-20-intelligent-design-plan.md` — why the system has this shape.
3. `scripts/operating-preflight.mjs` — dependency/repository/work-order readiness report; it writes nothing.
4. `scripts/project-pulse.mjs` — a read-only, human-review-oriented current-next report; it writes nothing and starts no background process.
5. `docs/` and source register — domain truth, sources, contracts, and physical evidence boundary.

### Before an agent works a work order

```text
1. Run node scripts/operating-preflight.mjs.
2. Read the work order and its stop rule.
3. Check branch, SHA, dirty state, and source hashes.
4. Work only inside the stated scope.
5. Run the exact exit tests.
6. Record observed versus proposed outcomes.
7. Do not commit, push, publish, spend, message, or create an account without current user authorization.
```

### Environment readiness

**Required now:** Node 20+, Git, the current repository, and the existing static fixture validator. No download is required to execute RG-01 through its local proof page prototype.

**Do not install yet:** React, three.js, R3F, PixiJS, GraphRAG, Neo4j, Cloudflare bindings, analytics SDKs, ad pixels, payment SDKs, Unity, Unreal, or any machine driver. Each has a clear later gate in the work-order data. Installing the future all at once would make the environment noisier, less reproducible, and harder to audit.

**Needed before physical/commerce release:** a documented materials/profile decision; an explicit purchase/delivery/rights policy; analytics/consent decision; chosen payment/fulfilment provider; current visual target; and authorization for any deployment, account creation, spend, analytics, or external message.

## Unnamed but important work

- **Rights and stewardship:** generation/source rights, artist attribution, customer license, resale allowance, and whether a family can be modified.
- **Material ethics:** source, waste, repair/reuse, packaging, local fabrication, and end-of-life as named choices rather than vague "regenerative" copy.
- **Evidence photography:** a canonical way to photograph a cut, registration, material, profile, and result so proof grows with every run.
- **Taste governance:** what makes a family recognizably Ritual Geometry without treating sacred language, scientific language, or cultural motifs as decorative extraction.
- **Accessibility:** readable static equivalents for motion, contrast, keyboard use, and a non-visual explanation of the assembly path.

## The simple answer

Ritual Geometry can become a rare kind of creative product: **a design house with receipts**. Its unusual power is not merely that it makes mandalas, knows math, emits vectors, or can someday talk to a loom. Its power is that it can make a beautiful object feel authored, explainable, responsibly reproducible, and alive with a real future.

The immediate job is to turn that possibility into one clear public offer and one measured learning loop. Everything more advanced should make that first loop sharper, safer, and more memorable.
