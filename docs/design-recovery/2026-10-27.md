## Repository-specific records

### D21 — Ritual Geometry: layered artifacts with an assembly story

**Use when:** When designing Ritual Geometry, coloring/stencil views, layered objects, GCode inspection, MachineJob exports, or its offers.

[Recovered intent] Ritual Geometry is its own authored product, not an Omni-Loom subproject. Its value is an artifact becoming understandable and desirable: family lineage, visibly distinct layers, registration, assembly, and a bounded takeaway. Discovery, understanding, and action reinforce one another.

[Design DNA] An exploded layered plate, attractive motif family, readable assembly choreography, coloring/stencil views, material/scale previews, and an inspectable proof rail. Preserve beauty and commercial clarity alongside evidence; a terminal-status dashboard is not the whole experience.

[Observed record] The README and operating plan describe a dependency-free atlas/GCode renderer and Quiet Bloom, Frequency Bloom, and Leaf Spiral draft fixtures. The operating plan is a dated local proposal and itself warns that local Proof Rail work was not yet pushed at that time. Do not promote that historical statement into today's remote state without checking.

[Concrete conflict] The plan records narrative draft → advisory → release versus schemas allowing draft/release/withdrawn. Resolve lifecycle semantics with a versioned migration decision before release labels proliferate.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Keep authored family identity separate from geometry invariants and product-neutral machine handoff. Use distinct interior geometry per layer, shared registration, declared coordinate frames, actual downloadable fixtures, and clear physical_unrun labels.

[Reimagined horizon] A proof-led studio/shop/teaching hybrid: digital layer packs, educator assembly experiences, commissioned objects, and later qualified manufacturing adapters. A bounded proof should make this product more intelligible, not shrink it to a GCode parser.

[Carry-forward test] The buyer/viewer can understand the stack, inspect a layer's job, match preview to exported paths, and see what remains physically unvalidated.
